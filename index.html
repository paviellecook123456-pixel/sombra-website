THE CHALLENGE

Your systems tell you pieces. Sombra connects the story.

Restaurant leaders often know something changed before they know why. Sombra is designed to connect the operational and financial signals that usually live in separate systems, reports and conversations.

01

Profit Leakage

Waste, purchasing, inventory discrepancies, labor, vendor issues and unexplained profitability changes quietly erode margin.

02

Operational Blind Spots

Sales may be down, labor may be high or inventory may be off while the relationship between those signals stays hidden.

03

Fragmented Accountability

Owners, GMs, chefs and managers may each hold one piece of the puzzle instead of one shared operational picture.
